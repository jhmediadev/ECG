Architectural Project Manager

The Architectural Project Manager acts as a representative of the company, and oversees the procurement and development of projects. Impacts the development of the project team and the project, while striving for excellence, demonstrating passion and integrity, empowering others, and collaborating with the team, client, and consultants.

Key Responsibilities:

  • Lead project planning and design phases, ensuring alignment with client goals and regulatory requirements.
  • Oversee the development of architectural drawings, renderings, and documentation.
  • Coordinate with consultants, contractors, and stakeholders to manage project scope, schedules, and budgets.
  • Conduct site visits to monitor progress and ensure construction aligns with design intent.
  • Ensure code compliance, permitting, and adherence to zoning regulations.
  • Manage client relationships and present design proposals and updates.
  • Supervise junior architects and support staff as needed.

Qualifications:

  • Bachelor’s or Master’s degree in Architecture or related field.
  • Licensed Architect (preferred but not always required).
  • 5+ years of architectural project management experience.
  • Proficiency in Smartsheets, Monday, AutoCAD, Revit, and other design software.
  • Strong leadership, communication, and organizational skills.
  • Knowledge of construction processes, building codes, and contract documentation.

If you are a motivated and detail-oriented individual with a passion for the construction industry, we encourage you to apply for this exciting opportunity. Join our team and contribute to our future success.
