Construction Billing Administrator

We are looking for a detail-oriented and organized Construction Billing Administrator to manage billing processes and financial documentation for our construction projects. The ideal candidate will have experience in the construction industry and be comfortable working in a fast-paced, deadline-driven environment.

Key Responsibilities: Prepare, review, and issue accurate and timely invoices for ongoing construction projects. Track and manage change orders, lien waivers, and pay applications. Coordinate with project managers and subcontractors to verify completed work and billing milestones. Maintain detailed billing records and ensure compliance with contract terms. Follow up on outstanding payments and assist with accounts receivable collections. Reconcile billing discrepancies and resolve issues with clients or internal teams. Assist with monthly financial reporting and job cost tracking. Qualifications: High school diploma or equivalent; Associate's or Bachelor’s degree in accounting or a related field preferred. 2+ years of billing or administrative experience in the construction industry. Familiarity with AIA billing, job costing, and contract billing formats. Proficiency in accounting or construction software (e.g., QuickBooks, Procore, Sage). Strong attention to detail, accuracy, and organization. Excellent communication and problem-solving skills.​

If you are a motivated and detail-oriented individual with a passion for the construction industry, we encourage you to apply for this exciting opportunity. Join our team and contribute to our future success.
