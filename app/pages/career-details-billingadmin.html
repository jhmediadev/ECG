Construction Billing Administrator

We are looking for a detail-oriented and organized Construction Billing Administrator to manage billing processes and financial documentation for our construction projects. The ideal candidate will have experience in the construction industry and will be comfortable working in a fast-paced, deadline-driven environment.

Key Responsibilities:

  • Prepare and process billing documentation for construction projects, including invoices, change orders, and progress billings.
  • Review contracts and project specifications to ensure accurate billing.
  • Maintain organized records of all billing-related documents and correspondence.
  • Collaborate with project managers and accounting teams to resolve billing issues.
  • Assist in the preparation of monthly financial reports and job cost analysis.
  • Track and manage change orders, lien waivers, and pay applications.
  • Coordinate with project managers and subcontractors to verify completed work and billing milestones.
  • Reconcile billing discrepancies and resolve issues with clients or internal teams.
  • Ensure compliance with company policies and industry regulations.


Qualifications:

  • High school diploma or equivalent; Associate's or Bachelor’s degree in accounting or a related field preferred.
  • 2+ years of billing or administrative experience in the construction industry.
  • Familiarity with AIA billing, job costing, and contract billing formats.
  • Proficiency in accounting or construction software (e.g., QuickBooks, Procore, Sage).
  • Strong attention to detail, accuracy, and organization.
  • Excellent communication and problem-solving skills.​


If you are a motivated and detail-oriented individual with a passion for the construction industry, we encourage you to apply for this exciting opportunity. Join our team and contribute to our future success.
